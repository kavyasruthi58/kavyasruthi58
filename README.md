## 👩‍💻 About Me

Hi, I'm **Kavya** — an AI & Data Engineer who loves turning complex problems into intelligent, real-world solutions.

My work spans **NLP**, **computer vision**, **healthcare analytics**, and **LLM-powered applications**. I care deeply about building systems that are not just accurate, but actually useful — production-ready, fast, and thoughtfully designed.

When I'm not training models or wrangling data, I'm exploring the latest in **agentic AI** and **MLOps** — always looking for the next interesting problem to solve.

> *I believe the best AI isn't the most complex — it's the one that makes someone's life a little easier.*
